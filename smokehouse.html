What We Do

The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse specializes in authentic, expertly smoked barbecue for events of all sizes. From intimate gatherings to large corporate functions, we bring exceptional flavor and professional service to every occasion.

Our Services

Catering

Full-service BBQ catering for weddings, corporate events, and private parties. Customizable menus featuring our signature smoked meats.

Private Events

Intimate gatherings with personalized service. Let us create an unforgettable dining experience for your special occasion.

Corporate Functions

Impress your clients and team with world-class BBQ. Perfect for team building and corporate celebrations.

Signature Offerings

  • Smoked Brisket - Slow-smoked for 12+ hours to perfection
  • Pulled Pork - Tender, flavorful, and fall-apart delicious
  • Smoked Ribs - Perfectly spiced and smoke-kissed
  • Smoked Chicken - Moist inside, smoky outside
  • Homemade Sides - Classic BBQ accompaniments
  • House Sauce - Our secret recipe that keeps people coming back

Our Philosophy

At The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse, we believe that great BBQ is more than just food—it's about bringing people together. Every event is treated with the same dedication to quality and excellence that defines our brand. We use traditional smoking techniques, premium ingredients, and passion in everything we do.
