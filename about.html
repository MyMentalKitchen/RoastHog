About Tim Clements

Meet the Pitmaster

Tim Clements is the founder and head chef of The Gipsy Hill Smokehouse, a catering business built on passion, dedication, and a love for authentic barbecue.

With years of experience perfecting his craft, Tim has developed a unique approach to smoking meats that combines traditional techniques with innovative flavors.
